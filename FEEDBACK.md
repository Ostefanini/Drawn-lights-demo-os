# Feedback

We welcome all feedback from users and contributors. Whether you spotted a bug, have a feature idea, a question, or simply want to share your experience — we want to hear from you.

---

## How to Share Feedback

Feedback is collected through our public feedback board, powered by UserJot.

**Access the feedback board:** [drawnlightsgame.userjot.com](https://app.userjot.com/cmpv6si161nie0ipjv3kgdhvz/d/requests)

You can also access it directly from the app by clicking the **"Donner un avis"** button in the bottom right corner of the interface.

The board is public — you can see what others have suggested, upvote requests you care about, and follow the status of your own submissions.

---

## What You Can Send Us

We encourage all types of feedback:

### 🐛 Bug Reports
Something isn't working as expected? Tell us what happened, what you expected, and how to reproduce it. Screenshots are always helpful.

### 💡 Feature Requests
Have an idea that would make the experience better? Describe what you'd like to see and why it matters to you. The more context, the better.

### ❓ General Questions
Not sure how something works? Ask away. Your question might help us improve the documentation or the interface.

### 👏 Compliments & Encouragements
Enjoying the project? We'd love to know what's working well. Positive signals help us understand what to double down on.

---

## Our Feedback Policy

### Response Time
We aim to acknowledge every piece of feedback within **48 hours**. This means you'll receive a confirmation that your feedback has been seen and is being considered.

### Processing Time
Once acknowledged, feedback is reviewed against the current roadmap and priorities. There is no fixed processing deadline — decisions are made based on:

- Impact on the user experience
- Alignment with the product vision
- Current development capacity
- Volume of upvotes from other users

Feedback that is accepted will be converted into a ticket and added to the roadmap. You'll be able to track its status directly on the feedback board.

### Feedback Statuses
| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Pending** | Received, not yet reviewed |
| **Reviewing** | Under consideration by the team |
| **Planned** | Accepted and added to the roadmap |
| **In Progress** | Actively being worked on |
| **Completed** | Shipped |

---

## Tips for Great Feedback

- **Be specific.** "The leaderboard doesn't load" is more actionable than "something is broken."
- **Include context.** Your browser, device, and steps to reproduce help us fix bugs faster.
- **Upvote existing requests.** If someone already submitted your idea, upvoting it helps us prioritize.
- **One topic per submission.** Separate feedback is easier to track and action.
