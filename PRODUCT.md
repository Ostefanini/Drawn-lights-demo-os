# Product Documentation

## Vision

TS - Drones is a minimalist, gamified demo of the Drawn Lights application.
It showcases Karine's product management practices and Oscar's engineering practices through a fully open source, publicly accessible project.

---

## Target Users

### Recruiters & Hiring Managers
People evaluating Karine and Oscar's professional profiles who want to see real product and engineering work in action — not just a CV.

**Goals:**
- Understand the product vision and scope quickly
- See evidence of structured product thinking (personas, roadmap, documentation)
- Explore a live, working application

**Pain points:**
- Portfolio projects are often too abstract or purely technical
- Hard to assess product management skills from a GitHub repo alone

---

### Open Source Contributors
Developers who discover the project and want to understand it well enough to contribute.

**Goals:**
- Understand what the project does and why it exists
- Identify where they can add value
- Get up to speed without asking the maintainers

**Pain points:**
- No documentation means inferring intent from source code
- Unclear priorities make it hard to know where to start

---

## Core Features

### Drone Show Catalog
Browse a collection of drone formations (cross, circle, triangle, square, and more), each with a visual preview, a description, and metadata (number of drones, duration, category).

### Show Composition
Combine multiple drone formations into a custom sequence to create a unique drone show experience.

### Audio Track Selection
Pair the drone show with an audio track to enhance the experience.

### Leaderboard
View and compare scores across players who have discovered exclusive drone show combinations.

### Multilingual Support
The interface is available in French and English.

### In-App Feedback
Users can share feedback, suggestions, and feature requests directly from the app via the integrated UserJot widget.
